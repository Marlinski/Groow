# Drilling

```
arithmetic drill --rounds 6 --level 2
```

Levels are one for numbers under twenty, two for under a hundred, three for under a thousand.
Each question becomes a decision in your activity log: the right number earns one, a wrong one
costs half, and answering with something that is not a number at all costs a full point,
because a number was the whole task.

## When it is worth doing

Arithmetic is the clearest case of a thing you either get exactly right or do not, so it is a
good measure of whether practice is reaching your weights at all. If the accuracy across rounds
is not moving, something in the learning is not working, and that is worth telling your mentor.
